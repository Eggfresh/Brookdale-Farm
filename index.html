Discover the Farm

A Place Apart

A hundred acres of living slowly

Tucked into the rolling countryside of Grey County, Brookdale Farm is a rare find — a working historic farmstead where guests are invited to breathe differently. Step off the porch and the pool glints through the trees. Walk further and a winding stream edges the far fields, blue herons drifting at dusk.


This is not a hotel. It is a proper farm — with a proper farmhouse, a red barn with string lights, Adirondack chairs set out by the water, and the particular quiet that only a hundred acres makes.

Our Story

Rooted in this land

Brookdale Farm has stood in Maxwell, Grey County for generations. The original farmhouse carries the quiet dignity of old Ontario — thick walls, windows that frame countryside the way paintings try to, and a red door that has welcomed people home for over a century.


Across a hundred acres of field and forest, the barn stands open with its timber frame and string lights — a gathering place as much as anything else. The stream has always been here. The herons found it long before us. We're simply grateful to share it all.

"Come as a guest.
Leave as someone who has exhaled."

Community Favourites

We've spent years in this corner of Grey County and have favourite spots around every bend. This is our honest, lovingly curated list — the places we'd send our own family to.

☕

Coffee & Bakeries

Good Grief Coffee Roasters↗

Thornbury's beloved independent roaster. Own-roasted beans, inventive baked goods — fresh donuts, PB&J cookie sandwiches — and a warm community vibe.

Thornbury
Ravenna General Store↗

A proper country general store five minutes from the farm. Stop for the atmosphere — and pick up a Kolapore trail map while you're there.

Ravenna · 5 min
Thornbury Bakery Café↗

Fresh bread daily, excellent pastries, and the best shortbread in Grey County. The kind of local bakery that anchors a small town.

Thornbury
Great Books & Café↗

Over 80,000 books in a 19th-century gristmill, plus excellent food. A full rainy afternoon very well spent.

Durham
🍽️

Restaurants & Dining

Down Home↗

A prix-fixe dinner in a cozy Markdale farmhouse with hyper-local, seasonal menus. Book well ahead — it sells out fast and is absolutely worth it.

Markdale · Must Book
The Gate↗

Fine dining in Flesherton with farm-to-table Mediterranean-inspired dishes that change with the seasons. A real surprise in a small town.

Flesherton
Marilynne↗

A warmly run Markdale favourite known for its weekend brunch, scratch-made with local farm ingredients.

Markdale · Brunch
Bruce Wine Bar↗

Local beers, curated wines, and wood-oven pizza in Thornbury. Feast On certified — everything sourced from Ontario growers.

Thornbury
Fabbrica Thornbury↗

Italian-inspired cooking in a relaxed Thornbury setting. A local favourite for dinner with a bottle of wine and no rush to leave.

Thornbury
🌲

Nature & Outdoor Activities

Eugenia Falls & Hogg's Falls Loop↗

A 12.6km loop through the Beaver Valley visiting two of Grey County's most striking waterfalls. Hogg's Falls has a hidden rope ladder to a swimming hole at its base.

Beaver Valley · Hike
The Bruce Trail — Beaver Valley Section↗

Ontario's famous long trail threads right through this region, with escarpment views, limestone caves, gorges, and valley panoramas for any ability level.

Nearby · Year-Round
Old Baldy Lookout↗

A short but rewarding hike to one of the finest escarpment views in Grey County. The entire Beaver Valley rolls out below — unmissable in fall colour season.

Kimberley · Views
Kolapore Uplands↗

Wild trails through the escarpment with a waterfall, rock scrambles, and a 30-metre gorge. Pick up a map at the Ravenna General Store first.

Ravenna · Trail Map Required
Feversham Gorge↗

The Beaver River cuts through a gorgeous gorge from an old mill dam. A short, rewarding hike with canyon views few expect in Southern Ontario.

Feversham
Blue Mountain Resort↗

Ontario's largest mountain resort — skiing in winter, hiking and mountain biking in summer, and a lively village year-round.

~45 min · Year-Round
🛍️

Shopping & Farm Experiences

The Cheese Gallery↗

A beloved artisan cheese shop in Thornbury with a vast selection and a brie grilled cheese sandwich that is genuinely hard to forget.

Thornbury
Secret Lands Farm↗

Friendly sheep, creamy sheep-milk cheeses, and a lovely on-farm store. A genuine farm stop that feels like stepping back in time.

Grey County · Farm Store
Kickin' Back Alpaca Ranch↗

Fluffy, curious alpacas that are immediately endearing. Farm store and animal meet-and-greets available.

Grey County · Family
Pom Pom Treat Hut↗

The perfect end to a day of exploring: excellent ice cream in a small-town setting that makes everything feel right with the world.

Thornbury · Ice Cream

Nearby Towns Worth Exploring

Thornbury↗

The charming anchor of the Beaver Valley — Ontario's Apple Capital, boutique shops, excellent restaurants, and Georgian Bay waterfront access.

~25 min
Markdale↗

Home of the original Chapman's Ice Cream — yes, that Chapman's — and some of the most well-regarded local dining in Grey County.

~15 min
Flesherton↗

A quiet village at the edge of the Beaver Valley with local shops and easy trail access. Great base for a morning hike before lunch at The Gate.

~20 min
Owen Sound↗

Grey County's largest town with a vibrant arts scene, Georgian Bay waterfront, and the Tom Thomson Art Gallery — he grew up here. Worth the drive.

~35 min
Eugenia & Kimberley↗

Two tiny Beaver Valley hamlets that feel barely changed in decades. The kind of places you stumble upon and want to stay forever.

~25 min · Scenic Drive
Collingwood↗

A lively Georgian Bay town with a great Saturday farmers' market, waterfront, and easy access to Blue Mountain.

~40 min

Seasonal Highlights

Apple Orchard Season

Grey County is Ontario's apple country. In late summer and fall, roadside stands and u-pick orchards appear around every bend.

August – October
Waterfall Season

Grey County's waterfalls are most dramatic in April, May, and early June when snowmelt has them roaring. Eugenia and Hogg's Falls are unmissable.

Spring · April–June
Fall Colour Drives

The Beaver Valley in October is one of the most spectacular autumn colour scenes in the province. Old Baldy on a clear October morning is unforgettable.

October
Autumn Salmon Run

Every fall, salmon run from Georgian Bay up the local rivers — a remarkable thing to witness. Ask us which creeks are running best that year.

September – October
Stargazing at the Farm

Maxwell's dark skies are extraordinary. Grab a blanket, walk into the field, and look up. That's all there is to it.

Right Here · Year-Round
Collingwood Farmers' Market↗

A superb Saturday market with local produce, baked goods, cheese, and artisan crafts. Stock up on provisions for the week at the farm.

Collingwood · Saturdays
